> Nonprofit academic medical center · Rochester, MN; Phoenix/Scottsdale, AZ; Jacksonville, FL
> #1 US hospital in multiple specialties · ~85,000 employees · $21.5B revenue (2025)

## Identity & Mission (use for: what is Mayo Clinic, where is it, who runs it)
- Private nonprofit academic medical center; 501(c)(3) since 1947
- Mission: "The needs of the patient come first"
- Three destination campuses: Rochester, Minnesota (flagship); Phoenix/Scottsdale, Arizona; Jacksonville, Florida
- Mayo Clinic Health System: affiliated community hospitals across Minnesota, Wisconsin, and Iowa
- CEO and President: Dr. Gianrico Farrugia
- Founded 1864 (William Worrall Mayo, M.D.); formally developed by the Mayo brothers in the 1880s-1890s

## Size & Scale (use for: how big is Mayo Clinic, employees, revenue)
- Nearly 85,000 employees worldwide (2025)
- Operating revenue: $21.5 billion (2025); $19.79 billion (2024)
- Net income 2025: $1.5 billion; operating income 2024: $1.3 billion (6.5% margin)
- 5.4 million outpatient visits (2024); 161,590 surgical cases (2025)
- Rochester hospital: 2,059 beds; 14 million square feet campus

## Rankings (use for: is Mayo Clinic the best, rankings, awards)
- US News Best Hospitals 2024-25: #1 in diabetes and endocrinology, gastroenterology and GI surgery, and pulmonology and lung surgery
- All three destination campuses on US News Honor Roll
- All three destination campuses hold Magnet nursing designation
- Joint Commission Gold Seal of Approval

## Research (use for: research, clinical trials, NIH funding, innovation)
- Total research investment 2024: $1.343 billion ($782.3M external, $539.5M internal)
- NIH funding FY2024: $264.9 million across 498 grants (~#35 nationally)
- Nobel Prize 1950: physicians Edward C. Kendall and Philip S. Hench for discovery of cortisone
- March 22, 1955: world's first successful open-heart surgery series (Dr. John W. Kirklin)
- Mayo Clinic Platform_Discover: 13.6+ million de-identified patients, 5.8+ billion images, 2.72+ billion lab results

## Medical School (use for: Mayo medical school, admissions, scholarships, tuition)
- Name: Mayo Clinic Alix School of Medicine (renamed after $200M gift from Jay Alix, 2019)
- Class size: ~105 students — Rochester (56) + Phoenix/Scottsdale (50)
- All incoming MD students receive full-tuition scholarship funded by the Jay Alix endowment
- Tuition: $67,900/year (2025-26) — covered by scholarship for all students
- Acceptance rate: approximately 3.4%
- Median MCAT percentile rose from 78th to 97th percentile (2014-2024)
