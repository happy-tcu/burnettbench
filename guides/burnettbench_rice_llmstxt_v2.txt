## Identity (use for: what is Rice, where is it, what kind of school)
- Legal name: William Marsh Rice University
- Private, nonprofit, nonsectarian research university
- Founded 1912; named for William Marsh Rice, Houston merchant whose bequest funded the university
- Located at 6100 Main Street, Houston, Texas 77005; 300-acre campus adjacent to the Texas Medical Center
- Total enrollment Fall 2025: ~8,880 students (~4,807 undergrad, ~4,264 graduate/professional)

## Rankings & Reputation (use for: is Rice a good school, rankings, reputation)
- US News National University Ranking: #17 (2026 edition), #16 (2027 edition)
- Acceptance rate: 8.0% (2025-26 cycle; 36,791 applications, 2,948 admitted)
- Student-to-faculty ratio: 6:1; 912 full-time faculty
- Nobel Prizes: Robert F. Curl Jr. and Richard E. Smalley (Rice faculty; 1996 Nobel Chemistry for buckminsterfullerene)

## Admissions (use for: how to get in, SAT, ACT, GPA, test policy)
- Acceptance rate: 8.0% (2025-26)
- SAT middle 50%: 1510-1560 composite; 740-770 EBRW, 760-800 Math
- ACT middle 50%: 34-36 composite
- Test-required for Fall 2026 entry (with hardship exceptions)
- 4-year graduation rate: approximately 85-87%
- Does not publish departmental or school-specific acceptance rates

## Cost & Financial Aid (use for: tuition, scholarships, financial aid)
- Undergraduate tuition and fees: $70,720 (2026-27)
- The Rice Investment:
  - Family income below $75,000 → no tuition, fees, room, or board
  - Family income $75,000-$140,000 → no tuition
  - Family income $140,000-$200,000 → at least half tuition covered
- Rice meets 100% of demonstrated financial need; NO loans in any need-based package (loan-free since Fall 2022)
- 45% of first-year students receive need-based aid; average net price ~$15,091
- Merit scholarships: all admitted first-year students automatically considered; flagship is the Trustee Distinguished Scholarship
- Total grant aid to undergraduates: ~$174 million projected FY2026

## Residential Colleges (use for: housing, residential life, O-Week)
- 11 residential colleges (12th, Chao College, under construction for 2026 opening)
- All entering students randomly assigned; remain college members for life
- Colleges are self-governing centers of social, intellectual, and dining life
- No traditional Greek life; residential colleges serve this social function

## Academics (use for: majors, programs, research)
- 8 schools: Humanities, Social Sciences, Natural Sciences, George R. Brown School of Engineering and Computing, Shepherd School of Music, Architecture, Jones Graduate School of Business, Glasscock School of Continuing Studies
- 29 PhD programs
- Research expenditures: $277.7 million (FY2024)
- Baker Institute for Public Policy: one of the nation's top-ranked university policy think tanks
- No law school or medical school

## Athletics (use for: sports, teams, conference)
- NCAA Division I-FBS; American Athletic Conference; nickname: Owls
