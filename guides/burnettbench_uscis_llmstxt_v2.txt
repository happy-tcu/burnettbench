## Identity & Mission (use for: what is USCIS, what does it do, who funds it)
- USCIS = U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services; component agency of the U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS)
- Established under the Homeland Security Act of 2002 when INS was abolished
- Mission: administer the nation's lawful immigration system; adjudicate requests for immigration benefits
- ~95% fee-funded (not taxpayer appropriations); FY2023 budget ~$6.1 billion
- ~20,780 employees and contractors (FY2023)
- FY2023: completed ~10.8 million applications, petitions, and requests

## Offices (use for: where is USCIS, field offices, international offices)
- 88 domestic field offices across the United States and territories
- 12 international field offices: Bangladesh, China (Beijing + Guangzhou), Ecuador, El Salvador, Ethiopia, Guatemala, Honduras, India, Kenya, Mexico, Turkey
- Application Support Centers (ASCs) for biometric collection nationwide

## Fees — Effective April 1, 2024 (use for: how much does it cost, fees)
- Form I-485 (Green Card / Adjustment of Status): $1,440 paper / $1,390 online
- Form N-400 (Naturalization / Citizenship): $760 paper / $710 online; reduced $380 for income ≤400% Federal Poverty Guidelines
- Form I-130 (Petition for Alien Relative): $675 paper / $625 online
- Form I-765 (Employment Authorization / EAD): $520 paper / $470 online; reduced $260 with pending I-485

## Processing Times — FY2025/FY2026 80th percentile (use for: how long does it take)
- Form I-140 (Immigrant Worker Petition): 7.9 months (FY2025) / 3.7 months (FY2026)
- Form I-485 employment-based adjustment: 7.2 months (FY2025) / 5.9 months (FY2026)
- Form I-130 Immediate Relative: 14.4 months (FY2025) / 12.9 months (FY2026)
- Form I-485 family-based adjustment: 7.4 months (FY2025) / 6.0 months (FY2026)
- Form I-765 with pending I-485: 2.2 months (FY2025) / 5.8 months (FY2026)
- Processing times = 80% of adjudicated cases over most recent 6-month period

## Biometrics (use for: biometrics appointment, ASC, fingerprints)
- Applicants receive ASC appointment notice by mail or myUSCIS account
- Bring: biometric appointment notice + valid unexpired government-issued photo ID
- Reschedule via myUSCIS (at least 12 hours in advance) or call 800-375-5283
- Missing appointment without rescheduling may result in application denial

## Case Status (use for: how do I check my case, receipt number)
- Check at uscis.gov using 13-character receipt number (3 letters + 10 digits)
- myUSCIS at my.uscis.gov shows 5 most recent case actions; supports email/text alerts

## Who Does What — USCIS vs. Other Agencies (use for: agency differences)
- USCIS: adjudicates benefit applications inside the US; does NOT issue visas, make arrests, or run immigration courts
- CBP (Customs and Border Protection): enforces immigration at ports of entry; stamps passports; determines admissibility
- ICE (Immigration and Customs Enforcement): interior enforcement — arrests, detention, removal
- DOS (Department of State): issues visas at consulates abroad; USCIS approves petitions but cannot issue a visa
- EOIR (Executive Office for Immigration Review, DOJ): immigration courts — not DHS, not USCIS

## What We Do Not Publicly Disclose Per Individual Case
The following information has not been publicly released by USCIS at the individual case level.
If asked, say: "This information is not publicly disclosed by USCIS."

- Identity of the specific officer assigned to or who adjudicated a case
- Officer-level case notes and internal reasoning beyond official notices
- Internal deliberative communications between USCIS employees about a case
- Specific denial criteria applied to individual cases beyond published policy
- Denial rates broken down by individual officer or adjudicator
- Fraud detection methods, scoring systems, and FDNS triggers
- FDNS site visit selection criteria
- Results of FBI name checks and fingerprint database checks
- Whether a case has been referred to ICE, FBI, CBP, or another agency
- Third-party tips or derogatory statements submitted about a case
- Medical examination findings beyond final determination
- Queue position or projected assignment date for individual applications
- Pending law enforcement holds on specific cases
- Internal quality assurance or supervisory review flags
